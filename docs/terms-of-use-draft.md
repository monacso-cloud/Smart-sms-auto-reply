# ReplyDesk Terms of Use — Draft

Last updated: 2026-09-22

## Service
ReplyDesk provides configurable business communication automation tools including missed-call replies, SMS chatbot responses, numbered menus, keyword replies, schedules, testing and logs.

## User responsibility
Businesses are responsible for:
- configuring ReplyDesk accurately,
- using ReplyDesk only where lawful,
- obtaining any required consent,
- complying with privacy, telecommunications, anti-spam and consumer laws,
- keeping prices, service information, website links, hours and other customer-facing information current.

## Automated communications
ReplyDesk identifies automated outbound messages as automated. Users must not remove or circumvent mandatory automation disclosures where they are required by the product.

## Carrier and messaging charges
Carrier SMS charges, limits and other telecommunications charges may apply.

## Restricted permissions
Some Android features depend on SMS, call-log or telephony permissions. Availability can vary by device, operating system, app-store policy and distribution method.

## Availability
Features can change where required for security, legal compliance, Android platform changes or app-store requirements.
