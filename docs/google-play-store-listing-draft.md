# ReplyDesk — Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App name
ReplyDesk

## Short description
Automated missed-call and customer SMS replies for busy businesses.

## Full description
ReplyDesk helps businesses respond when they cannot answer the phone or reply to messages immediately.

Core tools include:
- configurable missed-call auto replies,
- incoming SMS chatbot replies,
- custom numbered menus with up to 10 options,
- custom keyword matching,
- reply scheduling,
- Test Your Bot,
- call and auto-reply logs,
- configurable log retention,
- STOP / START handling,
- automated-message disclosure.

ReplyDesk is designed for many types of service businesses. Templates are editable and business-neutral.

Automated messages are clearly identified as automated.

Some Android telephony features may require sensitive permissions and may vary by device, operating system, distribution method and app-store policy.
