# ReplyDesk Privacy Policy — Draft

Last updated: 2026-09-22

ReplyDesk is a business communication automation application. This draft is for release preparation and must be reviewed before public publication.

## Information ReplyDesk may access
Depending on the features enabled and the Android distribution path, ReplyDesk may access:
- phone state needed to detect call status,
- call log information needed to identify missed calls and show call history,
- incoming SMS messages needed for chatbot replies,
- SMS sending capability needed to send automated replies,
- business configuration such as reply messages, keywords, menu options, schedules and retention settings.

## Why this information is used
The information is used only to provide features selected by the user, including missed-call auto replies, SMS chatbot replies, numbered menus, keyword matching, reply scheduling, testing and call/reply history.

## Automated-message disclosure
ReplyDesk labels automated outbound messages as automated so recipients are not misled into believing a human wrote the message.

## Call and reply logs
ReplyDesk-owned logs can be configured for 7, 14 or 30 days. Old ReplyDesk-owned logs can be deleted automatically. Users can also clear ReplyDesk-owned history manually. Clearing ReplyDesk history does not delete the phone's system call history.

## Phone numbers
ReplyDesk masks phone numbers where practical in its own log display.

## STOP and START
Where supported, recipients can use STOP to pause automated chatbot replies and START to enable them again.

## Backend and account data
When cloud account features are connected, ReplyDesk may store account, business settings, subscription entitlement and synchronised configuration on the ReplyDesk backend. The final published policy will describe the production hosting provider, retention and data processors.

## Data sharing
ReplyDesk does not sell personal information. The final policy will identify any service providers required for hosting, billing, messaging or app-store operation.

## Account deletion
Account deletion will be available through the ReplyDesk account flow once production sign-in and backend deployment are enabled.

## Security
ReplyDesk is designed to minimise stored sensitive information and restrict access to authenticated account data where backend services are used.
